❓ HOW TO PLAY

  • 100 questions · 4 rounds · 25 questions each. (100 câu, 4 vòng, mỗi vòng 25 câu)
  • You have 20 seconds for each question (30–45 seconds for Word Scramble, Matching, Sentence Ordering and Sound Sorting). (Hết giờ = tính là sai.)
  • Each correct answer gives +10 points. Maximum: 1000 points.
  • You can answer each question only once. The game moves to the next question by itself.
  • Fill in the blank: type the answer or tap a word, then press CHECK.
  • Matching: tap a word on the left, then its pair on the right.
  • Scramble / Ordering: tap the letters or words in the right order, then press CHECK.
  • When you finish, press REVIEW MISTAKES to learn from your errors and try them again!
